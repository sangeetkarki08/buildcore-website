Questions

Frequently asked

Does BuildCore need internet?

No. The program and your data stay on your own computer. Internet is only needed to join office and site computers, or for the optional AI assistant.

How many people can use it at once?

Everyone in the office connects to the main computer from a browser. For several companies, 200+ projects and 80+ people at once, BuildCore runs as a server edition on a cloud machine.

Will an update lose my data?

No. Every update backs up the program first, test-starts BuildCore on a copy of your data, and puts everything back by itself if anything fails. Records, numbers and accounts stay exactly as they were.

Can my accountant keep working the Tally way?

Yes. Accounts › Gateway (Tally-style) gives the familiar keys and vouchers (F4–F7, bill-wise, cost centres) on the same books.

Does it follow Nepali rules?

It uses the Nepali fiscal year from Shrawan 1, BS dates, VAT and TDS by section, NFRS fixed assets, DoR / DUDBC norms, and FIDIC / PPMO claims.

What if a site has no internet?

The site sends a pack file by pen drive or e-mail; head office checks it and loads it into the main data.

I forgot my password.

Click Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. Use your recovery code, a reset code from your administrator, or, for the administrator, the master key file or a supplier reset key. It works offline.
