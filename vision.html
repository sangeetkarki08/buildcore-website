The problem we started from

A contractor in Nepal often learns the profit of a project a year late and finds a cash gap only when salaries are due. The site has the facts, the store has the facts, accounts has the facts, but they never meet.

Vision

Profit and cash known while there is still time to act

BuildCore's vision is a contracting company where the site, the store, the commercial team, the machines and the accountant work on the same numbers on the same day. Profit is known per BOQ item every month, cash is visible thirteen weeks ahead, and every figure can be traced back to the work that produced it.

Mission

Give every Nepali contractor big-company control, without big-company cost or complexity.

  • Works on the computers you already have
  • Familiar to site people and to Tally accountants
  • Grows from one office to a cloud server for 80+ users
  • Learnable from a video course and a 68-page guide
Principles

Six rules every release follows

One database, one truth

Site, store, billing, machines, cash and accounts share one ledger and one stock. There is no second copy to reconcile, so the books always agree with the site.

Nothing typed twice

Each step starts from what the step before produced. A DPR becomes progress, cost and logsheet; a GRN becomes stock and a liability; an IPC becomes the invoice.

Made for Nepal

Nepali fiscal year from Shrawan 1, BS dates and Nepali-month logsheets, VAT and TDS by section, NFRS assets, DoR and DUDBC norms, FIDIC and PPMO claims.

Your data stays yours

It runs on your own computers and works with no internet. Office, sites and the cloud are optional, never required.

Change without fear

Audit and report before building. New rules start switched off on existing data until accounts turns them on. Cancel, never delete. Every update is tested on a copy of your data.

Simple for each person

A storekeeper sees store screens, an accountant sees accounts, a director sees decisions. Familiar menus that stay where people expect them.
