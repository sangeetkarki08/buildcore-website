Release history

From foundation to finance platform

Nov 20262.48

Informative dashboard

Needs attention grouped as Important, This week, Review and For info; contract notices only for employer and neutral delays; a This month panel with work done, billed, received, paid and cost.

Nov 20262.47

BOQ by location

A short code on every BOQ line made of location and item (HW-M25); summary by item; BOQ import with locations; pickers show the codes.

Nov 20262.46

Tally-style accounting

Accounts Gateway with 28 groups over the chart, voucher types and numbering, F4–F7 entry with bill-wise and cost centres, Tally trial balance, P&L, balance sheet and day book, audit lock date and Edit Log.

Nov 20262.45

Three-way match

Site receipts, the vendor's VAT bill as printed and payments linked per vendor, including bills printed with another item and purchases without a VAT bill; Bill vs actual.

Nov 20262.44

People and access

One Users screen with a job and where each person works; each job sees only its modules; project teams with Copy team; See / Enter / Approve rights.

Nov 20262.43

Past prices

Purchase request and order rates fill from the project's posted goods receipts, with a past prices panel.

Nov 20262.42

Honest profit

Forecast profit counts only BOQ items with a cost estimate, and work in hand warns when the BOQ and contract value differ.

Nov 20262.38–2.41

Books agree, money end to end

One depreciation per machine and month, one project number on every screen, machines from bills, wages from the DPR, salaries, petty cash, bank import, loan register, papers with transactions.

Oct–Nov 20262.34–2.37

Library, claims, offline password, speed

Document library and claims register, DPR machine hours that fill themselves, forgot password offline, company-wide screens from 623 s to 20 s, server edition.

Oct 20262.30–2.33

Reports and machines

Page fit and Nepali months, a report generator with 42 sources, monthly logsheets by Nepali month, hours and output per machine.

Oct 20262.25–2.29

Universal DPR and cash control

One DPR per project, date and shift with reported vs accepted quantities; IPC to cash; 13-week cash forecast; DPR to cost and cost to complete; financial command centre with 16 alerts.

Oct 20262.19–2.24

Foundation for control

Procurement split, role-based login and workspaces, approve-then-post queue, subcontract work orders, equipment cost per hour, segment tags, cost codes, control budget, monthly close and CVR.

Before Oct 20261.6–2.18

The core

Site diary, BOQ, store, purchasing, billing, equipment and double-entry accounts in one database on your own computer.
